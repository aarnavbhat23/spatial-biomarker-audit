## Claims supported now

- Fixed-graph marker-error sensitivity can be bounded, with exact checks and
  retained patient-level outcomes under explicit budgets.
- Feasible opposite-sign categorical assignments establish ambiguity for some
  previously examined cases under their declared label-error contracts.
- The new discrete position oracle enumerates all allowed worlds on small
  examples. Its bounds are verified but too wide in the scaling screen.
- The preceding finite acquisition study has a strong greedy control that
  matches its six optima. It establishes no acquisition-algorithm advantage.
- Public measured-geometry and expert-disagreement studies expose the gap between
  a declared error budget and a defensible biological guarantee.

Neither expert disagreement nor simulated pixel perturbations are adjudicated
biological truth. Conditional sensitivity intervals and frequentist sampling
intervals answer different questions; narrower intervals are not automatically
better if the uncertainty sets or targets differ.

## The next work worth doing

1. Select one target marker pair and radius with an imaging collaborator. Assemble
   target-matched independent identity and geometry adjudications. Record missing
   and merged cells, not just coordinate jitter. Existing public disagreement
   data do not establish the required truth contract.
2. Freeze calibration/held-out patient splits, error-budget construction, a
   biologically meaningful contrast tolerance, and a minimum useful-certification
   rate before seeing held-out outcomes. Keep the independent cohort untouched
   until this measurement definition is viable.
3. Evaluate coverage and interval width together. Compare random review, the
   existing strong greedy rule, and conventional spatial inference on the same
   estimand; state explicitly when a method does not cover measurement error.
4. Only after useful certificates exist, measure review time, scan time and
   feedback latency in the lab pilot. Simulated static costs are not scanner
   savings. If adjudicated truth remains unavailable, publishable scope is a
   conditional sensitivity benchmark, not a clinically validated method.

No additional graph-bound variants are recommended until a calibrated error
model explains why they would produce useful intervals. The exact oracle remains
valuable for rejecting unsafe future methods and distinguishing relaxation
looseness from intrinsic ambiguity. Wide outer bounds alone do not prove that
the actual identified set is wide at scale.
